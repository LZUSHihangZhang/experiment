	我们测定数据为
	\[
	\begin{array}{|c|c|}
		\hline
		x_1 \text{ (mm)} & x_2 \text{ (mm)} \\
		\hline
		20.125 & 17.758 \\
		\hline
	\end{array}
	\]
	我们实验采用的玻璃片和三棱镜实验所用的三棱镜折射率相同,从而我们可以测量到$n = 1.516$\\
	我们可以计算得到
	\[d = \frac{|x_2-x_1|}{20\times(n-1)} = 0.22936046511627914mm\]
	我们可以计算B类不确定度.\\
	对于我们的实验
	\[u_B(x_1) = \frac{0.01mm}{\sqrt{3}} = 0.005773502691896258mm\]
	\[u_B(x_2) = \frac{0.01mm}{\sqrt{3}} = 0.005773502691896258mm\]
	我们的数据$x_1>x_2$,则
	\[d = \frac{x_1-x_2}{20\times(n-1)}\]
	\[\frac{\partial d}{\partial x_1} = \frac{1}{20\times(n-1)} = 0.09689922480620154\]
	\[\frac{\partial d}{\partial x_2} = \frac{1}{20\times(n-1)} = 0.09689922480620154\]
    \[u(x_1)=u_B(x_1),u(x_2)=u_B(x_1)\]
    则我们可以计算得到
    \[U(d) = d\sqrt{(\frac{\partial d}{\partial x_1})^2u(x_1)^2+(\frac{\partial d}{\partial x_2})^2u(x_2)^2} = 0.00018146515074363845mm\]
    则\[d = (0.2294\pm0.0002)mm\]
